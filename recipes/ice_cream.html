Ice-Cream Recipe

The simplest method for making homemade ice cream is the "no-churn" method. It requires no special equipment—only 3 main ingredients and about 10 minutes of prep.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml heavy cream
  • 200 ml condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Whip the heavy cream until it becomes thick and fluffy.
  2. Add condensed milk, vanilla extract, and salt, then mix gently.
  3. Pour the mixture into a container and smooth the top.
  4. Freeze the ice cream for at least 4–6 hours.
  5. Let it sit for a few minutes before serving.
