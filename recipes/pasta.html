Pasta Recipe

Boil 4 to 6 quarts of water in a large pot. Once at a rolling boil, add 1 to 2 tablespoons of salt. Drop in your pasta, stir immediately to prevent sticking, and cook for 1 minute less than the package instructions so it finishes al dente

ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (optional)
  • Parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Pasta sauce (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Add the pasta to the boiling water and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Stir occasionally to prevent the pasta from sticking together.
  4. Check if the pasta is cooked to your preferred texture.
  5. Drain the pasta and add olive oil or sauce if desired.
  6. Serve the pasta with Parmesan cheese or your favorite toppings.
