Lasagna Recipe

In this classic lasagna recipe, sheets of pasta are layered with a cheesy filling, a rich meaty tomato sauce, and more cheese and then baked until bubbly and browned.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef (or mixed ground beef and pork)
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 ml tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 250 g lasagna sheets
  • 500 ml milk
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g all-purpose flour
  • 150–200 g shredded mozzarella (or Gouda)
  • 50 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Oregano
  • Basil
  • Nutmeg (for the bechamel sauce)

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce by cooking the onion and garlic, then add the ground meat and cook until browned.
  2. Add tomato sauce and tomato paste, season with salt, pepper, oregano, and basil, then simmer for about 20–30 minutes.
  3. Prepare the béchamel sauce by melting butter, adding flour, and slowly mixing in milk until it becomes smooth and creamy.
  4. Assemble the lasagna by layering meat sauce, béchamel sauce, lasagna sheets, and cheese.
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, then finish with cheese on top.
  6. Bake the lasagna in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for about 30–40 minutes.
  7. Let the lasagna rest for a few minutes before serving.
